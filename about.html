About Us

Dinkapedia the language learning website

Dinkapedia's goal is to provide both information and resources for immersing into the Dinka Language. Understanding the Dinka tribe has many dialects, accents and clans, this platorm tries to diversify there content and does not claim to be a standard form of language, but a step into becoming fluent.

Learning how to speak

The goals of the learning section are to hear how others speak in Dinka, and the basics of how to say things from English. It provides quizzez, videos, and lesson paths with different units, . The end goal is to be able to communicate with Dinka people, it will include slang, dialects, and words used in other languages.
